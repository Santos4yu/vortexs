What earns a place

A read must survive the whole matchup.

Recent hit rate starts the conversation. Pitch type, handedness, contact shape and game environment decide whether the signal holds up.

01

Starter and arsenal

ERA and WHIP sit beside pitch mix, K/9, HR/9 and how the batter handles the pitches they will actually see.

02

Batter shape

L5, L10 and L20 form are checked against hard-hit rate, barrels, platoon splits and lineup position.

03

Full-game environment

Park, wind, temperature, bullpen quality, fatigue and defensive context can strengthen—or break—the case.

04

Visible risk

Missing samples and conflicting factors are shown, not buried. VORTEX is designed to pass when the evidence does not align.
